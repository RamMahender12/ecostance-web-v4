# EcoStance website brief

## 1. Product focus
Build a product-focused website (not services-led). Get inspired by Altana.ai. Use EcoStance.com template for content seeds. Prefer product images over people/service stock. Use EcoStance product images from test.ebcopen.com.

## 2. Traceability, not sustainability-only
The website must be not just for sustainability but also for product traceability like Altana (very important).

## 3. Audience and vertical
Target brands and businesses that want product and supply-chain traceability. The focus is clothing, wool sweaters, fashion.

### 3a. Customer connection
Better connection with customers: transparency on product and material sourcing, and that the supply chain complies with ESG (no child labour, fair wages, environmental stewardship in farming and manufacturing).

### 3b. Regulations
Comply with Europe’s EUDR and others — not EUDR only. Include ESPR/DPP, CSRD, CSDDD, CBAM, Forced Labour Regulation, PPWR, UFLPA, customs/origin.

## 4. AI-native company
Position EcoStance as an AI-native company with a trained LLM and pre-built end-to-end workflows. Competitive advantage: Native AI delivers with speed and lower cost; pre-built workflows simplify the end-to-end experience. Include an AI-native conversational interface on the website.

### AI-native advantages
- **4a)** Rapidly calculate carbon footprint vs months/years of data collection
- **4b)** Pre-built workflows for disclosure reporting — nothing manual — resulting in trust
- **4c)** All transactions backed by untampered immutable machine-generated certificates (100% authenticity from an immutable ledger) — critical to creating trust
- **4d)** Data layer with secure observability and data controls for end-to-end supply-chain transactions; brands use data for sustainability and product traceability; data surfaces in Digital Product Passport
- **4e)** Chain-of-custody traceability for raw material surfaced in Digital Product Passport

## 5. Products today
- **5a)** Digital Product Passport — flagship; prominent on homepage including slider image
- **5b)** Supply chain and product traceability (suppliers + distributors) — second most important
- **5c)** Carbon offset purchase / carbon neutral with disclosure reporting; own carbon exchange
- **5d)** Carbon footprint calculations for individuals, businesses, events — minutes not hours/weeks
- **5e)** Employee engagement — calculate emission footprint and join sustainability initiatives
- **5f)** Green events — conferences/events with participant offsetting
- **5g)** Cross-border commerce — side reference only (not homepage hero): transaction traceability, logistics traceability, identity, data and auditability for raw-material trade

## 6. Quick Start Services
Product company first. Offer 5 one-week pilots + free strategy (never call this “Foot-in-the-door”):
- **6a)** DPP Pilot Launch — Digital Product Passports for a small SKU set
- **6b)** Carbon Neutral Kickstart — footprint → offsets → disclosure page
- **6c)** EUDR QuickStart Disclosures
- **6d)** Supply Chain Proof Pack — few suppliers, show transparency
- **6e)** Engagement Starter — partners, suppliers, employees
- **6f)** Free Strategy Session — up to 3 hours
